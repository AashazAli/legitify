**One final tip:** Figma Make is excellent for generating an initial concept, but it won't produce a truly distinctive enterprise product in a single pass. The strongest results come from generating the design system first, then iteratively refining individual modules (Mission Control, Investigation Workspace, Case Management, Analytics, etc.) until they share a consistent visual language. That's how products at large organizations are actually designed.
