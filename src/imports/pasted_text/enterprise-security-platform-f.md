### One important product recommendation

Avoid trying to make **every page** look as dense as a security operations center. Different users have different needs.

A stronger enterprise product has **role-based experiences**:

* **Students** get a clean, guided scan-and-result flow.
* **Placement officers and recruiters** get verification workflows, analytics, and organization management.
* **Security analysts and administrators** get the full Mission Control experience with investigation tools, telemetry, and advanced analytics.

This gives you both an approachable product for everyday users and a powerful enterprise platform for organizations—making LEGITIFY more practical to adopt while still feeling like a premium enterprise system.
