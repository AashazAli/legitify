# LEGITIFY — DESIGN THE NEXT-GENERATION TRUST INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

You are not designing a UI.

You are designing the future of enterprise trust verification.

LEGITIFY is an Enterprise Trust Intelligence Platform that enables organizations to verify companies, recruiters, internships, job offers, domains, websites, emails, documents, and digital identities before trust decisions are made.

Imagine LEGITIFY becoming the "CrowdStrike of Recruitment Trust", the "Stripe Radar for Hiring", or the "GitHub Copilot for Trust Decisions."

The product should be capable of scaling from:

* Individual students
* Universities
* Placement cells
* HR teams
* Recruitment agencies
* Startups
* Fortune 500 companies
* Governments
* Cybersecurity organizations
